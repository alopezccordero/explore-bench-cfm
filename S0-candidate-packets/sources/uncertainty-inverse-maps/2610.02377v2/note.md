## Full-text-supported observations and limits

Inspected §§1, 3 accuracy-estimate/hybrid-sampler framing, §4 experiment descriptions, §5 limitations (paragraphs 5–73; raw text 5955, 7417, 9703). Conditional FM compared to MCMC on PDE inverse problems including EIT and likelihood-free Lotka–Volterra. Computable TV/KL accuracy estimates and independent Metropolis correction require evaluable flow densities and likelihood conditions; asymptotic exactness does not mean finite-budget exact maps. Accuracy degrades for concentrated low-noise posteriors and prior-predictive tail observations. This is a strong negative-result precedent: amortization can lose reliability precisely where the posterior concentrates.

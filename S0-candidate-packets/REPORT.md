## D — Matched marginals, uncertain dependence
**RQ:** Under a fixed true distribution of hidden-door grid worlds, when do finite joint map hypotheses lower or raise one-probe capped navigation cost relative to doorwise-shuffled hypotheses with exactly the same empirical marginals, as sample count, joint-mode misspecification and probe cost vary?

**Minimum decisive study:** declare valid room/door families and common truth P; compare correct joint samples, finite samples, and wrong joint Q with matched population marginals; use independent permutations of whole-door columns for the empirical-marginal intervention. Keep decision functional, legal query set, continuation and budgets fixed. Include exact same-prior and no-query controls; score all failures with the prespecified cap. Independent unit is map family, not doors or rollouts. Finite permutations do not guarantee exact independence.

**Why worth asking despite prior work:** a precise replication can separate representation loss from finite support and wrong dependence, instead of comparing different planners with different information. A reversal can show when preserving the wrong relationships is worse than discarding them. It cannot discover that correlated doors matter—that is already explicit in **LAMP (2012.02271v1)** and **Lamarre & Kelly (2505.13674v2, §5.2.3)**. Bayesian Active Edge Evaluation also has a same-world-matrix marginal baseline and structured-world regimes. Full-text notes: `sources/synthesis-0252/{lamp,risk-correlated}/note.md`, `sources/synthesis-0322/bisect/note.md`.

**New round006 counterevidence (~04:46 CST):** **Zhou & Ceyhan, Stochastic Path Planning in Correlated Obstacle Fields (2509.19559v2)** already supplies an actual2D-grid correlation-aware traversal study (§8), correlation versus independence DT example (§8.3), and an optimal-policy coarsening-dominance theorem (§4.4.1). Thus neither2D scope nor a correct-joint advantage separates D. Its A3 coarsens the UPDATED exact posterior; this is not updating an independent prior. D's evaluator now explicitly shuffles BEFORE the prospective reveal and lets each arm update its own belief; different subsequent marginals are intended consequences, not a reason to re-match away the effect. A product of exact posterior marginals remains a stronger diagnostic, not a silently substituted baseline. **Bampis et al., Canadian Traveller Problem with Predictions (2209.11100v1, §1.1)** also already studies prediction consistency/robustness, with a different worst-case estimand. Neither inspected source establishes the exact finite-N/matched-empirical-marginal/wrong-Q factorial, but this bounded finding does not prove novelty. D remains conditional replication only, not an upgraded leader. Source notes: `sources/synthesis-0440/{scos,predictions}/note.md`; new SCOS seed resolves in OpenAlex, S2 batch429 remains unresolved.

**Explain to Dr. Enriquez:** “I want to measure the boundary where keeping joint map structure is worth its approximation risk, while holding marginal accuracy at the pre-query intervention and the algorithm's decision rule fixed. The negative result is a concrete regime where the simpler belief is sufficient or safer.”

## B2 — Where should temporal stability live?
**RQ:** On small partially observed grid-navigation tasks with equal completion samples and planning budgets, can a replaceable speculative map plus route-switch hysteresis match or reduce capped navigation cost relative to validation-selected clamped prediction accumulation with observed-cell overwrite safeguards, as completion volatility and newly revealed contradictions vary?

**Minimum decisive study:** cross replaceable/accumulated map representation with hysteresis off/on; both systems can use route memory and strong safeguards. Use the same static world population, legitimate reveals, sample budgets and immediate known-obstacle veto. Different routes can legitimately reveal different cells; do not force identical closed-loop observations after trajectories diverge. Completion tapes in the minimum are finite-prior samples, not learned FM results. Score blocked attempts and non-goal termination, not only successful paths. CPU/deadline/tuning limits are stipulations needing confirmation.

**Why interesting:** stability may avoid route reversals, but memory may delay useful correction. The ranking is not fixed by the elementary fact that repeated predictions are not independent evidence. **SceneSense §6** explicitly motivates persistence by oscillatory replacement behavior; **OctoMap §3.2** already clamps confidence. **PACT (2609.01662v2)** already separates evidential origin, repeated inference and action utility. Do not claim a deployed SceneSense defect or guess its reset/clamp defaults. Evidence and exact limitations: `recovery_reviews/B/review.md`; local full-text notes in the packet's seed file.

**Explain:** “Rather than rediscover double counting, I will compare two places to store temporal memory—belief versus planner—and measure the navigation tradeoff under fair safeguards.”

## C2 — Observation-policy transfer in a trained conditional FM
**RQ:** For a small conditional FM map-completion sampler, does replacing independent random-mask training records with records from a stipulated noiseless local-reveal process reduce joint posterior discrepancy on the same fixed local-reveal test population, at equal examples, parameters, updates and sampling effort?

**Minimum decisive study:** proposed 64-world 8×8 catalog, known prior and transparent reveal likelihood, paired learned models and seeds. Both arms train/evaluate on the same catalog: this is **known-support conditional estimation, not held-out-world generalization**. First-hit grid rays are an optional simulator rule, not sensor realism or a verified existing constraint. Use the actual simulator rule if available and scientifically suitable; changing it requires updating the likelihood and packet before freezing. Do not add post-hoc noise to clean stopping masks. Under the declared noiseless nonadaptive rule mask+values suffice; prove this rather than calling obstacle-dependent visibility automatically MNAR.

**New independent adversarial finding:** CFMI **§3.2 explicitly already says an effective splitting strategy should align with true missingness**, and uses Random-Historical splitting. Observation-Aligned Mask Priors already compares heuristic and authentic mask strategies. Therefore mask matching is not an unclaimed idea; only the grid-specific finite-budget joint-distribution audit remains. Sources independently retrieved/read this round: `sources/synthesis-0410/{cfmi,mask-priors}/note.md`.

**Score defect caught before selection:** the reviewer draft's characteristic exp(-Hamming/64) kernel plus +/-0.01 band can call severe correlation error negligible. A two-ambiguous-cell perfect-correlation versus independent-marginal example has population MMD² only **0.00006009**. This is algebra, not an experiment. New packet declares **0.5 equality kernel + 0.5 smooth Hamming kernel**, producing **0.125030045** for that anchor, changes signed direction to maximize matched-training benefit, and withdraws accepted-equivalence claims until margin justification. Finite-K error, absolute errors, outside-catalog mass and failure tokens remain mandatory. The replacement is explicit before selection, not a result-driven metric swap. Full derivation: `sources/synthesis-0410/analytic-instrument-audit.md`.

**Why a null matters:** random-mask training might already cover the required conditionals at this budget; matched data generation may not earn its cost. Both models can be equally bad, so zero contrast alone is not success. Stop if oracle correctness, instrument sensitivity, small-model learning or allowed compute fails. No training occurred tonight.

**Explain:** “I want to test whether the simulator's way of revealing cells changes what a fixed small CFM learns, using an exact reference that detects whole-map mistakes rather than only pixel error. This is a controlled replication with a useful null, not a new imputation method.”

## A — Connectivity is not the same as route value
**RQ:** For finite grid populations, when does selecting one equal-cost reveal by information about binary start–goal reachability lower or raise capped navigation cost relative to cell variance, world information and same-prior expected-cost acquisition, as reachability uncertainty and route-cost ambiguity vary separately?

A known fallback makes reachability certain while shortcut value remains unknown. That zero-information result is a required analytic control, not a research outcome. Require non-tautological held-out boundary cases and credible low-cost proxy value, otherwise reject A. **MacDonald & Smith (DOI 10.1177/0278364918772024)** and **Bayesian Active Edge Evaluation (1711.07329v1)** already establish much of the surrounding task-aware acquisition argument. Notes: `sources/synthesis-0322/{macdonald,bisect}/note.md`; MapEx note `sources/robotic-mapping/mapex-original/note.md`.

**Explain:** “I want to find when asking whether *any* route exists is enough, versus when the algorithm must ask which route is worth using.” Minimum does not require FM.

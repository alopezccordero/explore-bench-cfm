## Round 02: decisive instrument corrections (2026-11-16 ~03:00 CST)
These override any generic shared-template wording above. Minimum D is a **controlled replication/robustness audit**, not evidence that door correlations can help (already established by LAMP and correlated CTP).

1. **Fixed evaluation population:** define a finite true world prior P per layout and use it to generate/evaluate ALL methods' episodes. Alternative assumed joint distributions Q share P's door marginals but may misallocate joint modes. Priors, observation kernels and geometry are declared, not learned from test truth. Never evaluate Q-policy cost on Q worlds and P-policy cost on P worlds: that changes the estimand.
2. **Separate two ablations:** (a) exact enumerated P versus the exact product of its marginals, a mechanism ceiling/reference; (b) a finite matrix of Q samples versus independent whole-door column permutations of that SAME matrix, matching empirical marginals and N exactly. Vary N and prespecified Q misspecification separately; the exact product of empirical marginals diagnoses residual permutation dependence. A permutation is not a proof of finite-sample independence. Include matched independent-world and task-irrelevant-door negative controls.
3. **Policy identifiability:** same one-probe selection algorithm, candidates, sensor observation model, tie breaks, and continuation rule for every representation. Add no-probe as a legitimate action. Do not interpret different solver quality or free posterior access as a dependence effect. Sensor costs include getting to a physically feasible probe; observations are generated by P's common sensor process. Freeze a zero-support observation fallback before testing and count its frequency, rather than drop rare-mode failure cases.
4. **Concrete primary score:** for each episode, let L be total grid-distance-equivalent cost of sensing travel plus executed navigation and recovery. Let H be a fixed, family-normalized cost cap chosen from geometry before held-out effects are seen. Score L if goal reached with L < H; otherwise score H, including timeout, invalid output, no-route, abstention and disconnected truth. At L = H score H. Use the same H for paired methods; publish success and failure categories separately because capped cost compresses catastrophic failures. Families may be normalized by a declared scale before H is fixed, not by a method-specific route. Compute equal episode means per independent map-family instance and equal cluster means for the preregistered pooled result. The primary paired contrast is joint minus shuffled: negative favors joint. No success-conditioned average.
5. **Boundary, not post-hoc crossover:** predeclare a small finite grid of joint-mode error × sample budget × probe cost. Report paired cluster intervals and MC variability for every cell, plus a frozen pooled contrast. A useful null requires an advisor-chosen practical margin and adequate interval precision; nonsignificance does not establish adequacy of marginal maps.

## Three curiosity sentences
A map-completion model may learn from scattered revealed cells but later see what a simulator reveal rule reveals. Those two ways of observing the same place may teach different beliefs about hidden obstacles. I want to know whether matching the training observations to the declared reveal rule actually improves the model's whole-map beliefs, rather than merely its pictures.

## Precise research question
For a conditional flow-matching (CFM) sampler trained on complete maps from a stipulated finite occupancy-world population, does replacing independent random-mask training records with noiseless first-hit ray training records reduce joint posterior discrepancy on one fixed ray-observation test population, at the same training-example, parameter, optimization and inference budgets?

Primary estimand: Delta = E[MMD_k^2(Q_random(.|O), P(.|O)) - MMD_k^2(Q_ray(.|O), P(.|O))], averaging over independent training seeds and O drawn from the fixed true ray-observation process. Positive Delta means matched-ray training is better. The revised signed metric is maximized to orient positive effects toward matched-policy benefit; it is not an absolute competence score. Both arms are mandatory actually trained CFM models. The exact posterior is an evaluator, not a third learned method.

## Population and unit
Minimum: one frozen, explicitly enumerated uniform catalog of 64 distinct binary 8x8 maps, generated from small room/corridor templates, with fixed boundary cells. Both training and evaluation draw from this SAME catalog; no unseen-layout/general-world generalization claim. Freeze catalog, prior and pose distribution using development-only checks before final training. Each ray starts at a catalog-common free boundary cell, with cardinal direction sampled independently of hidden world; reveal traversed free cells and first occupied cell, or boundary termination. Observation includes pose/direction, revealed mask, values and termination. Primary observation bundle: one ray per record. Reject the proposed catalog at construction if all posteriors are degenerate or identical to the prior; select a nontrivial family on development, never final results. Such selection defines the population, not evidence of effect.

Independent replication unit is a paired training seed; test records are nested draws, not independent trained-model replications. Use the same 512 held-out random observation draws in both arms for each of five paired seeds; train/development/test RNG streams are disjoint. Repeated catalog worlds and coincident records are permitted and disclosed: this is conditional-distribution estimation on known support, not a held-out-world benchmark.

## Intervention and fairness
Random arm: independent Bernoulli revelation per nonboundary cell, probability fixed using development-only mean revealed-cell fraction under the ray process. Matched arm: the exact ray process. Both receive complete-map supervised targets and complete legitimate observation inputs, but never hidden test labels as conditioning. Use cell values and mask as learned context for BOTH arms; omit pose/direction, termination flags and mechanism code as learned features in the noiseless nonadaptive minimum. Keep the full record in evaluator audit logs. Under this declared reveal rule, prove mask+values sufficiency as described in the round005 analytic audit; if sufficiency fails for the actual simulator rule, rederive the likelihood and provide all necessary observable context identically in both arms. No architecture/hyperparameter differences; paired initial weights, training world streams, number of examples and updates. Record actual runtimes. The contrast bundles spatial structure, observation counts and informative stopping; it does NOT identify geometry alone or intrinsic FM-specific failure. Mean-count matching is not information matching. No per-map count-bin rejection.

## Explanations and informative outcomes
Matched training may improve approximation on structured records; random masking may already cover the relevant conditionals sufficiently; finite training/integration/decoding error may dominate either arm. Positive Delta supports a bounded training-policy transfer penalty; near-zero with an adequately narrow interval supports practical insensitivity only at the frozen resolution/budget; a wide null is inconclusive; negative Delta suggests random-mask regularization or easier optimization wins here. Neither sign proves a deployment need, a general MNAR effect, or unique FM mechanism. Inspect oracle-sampling and independent-marginal controls to test whether the instrument can detect joint errors.

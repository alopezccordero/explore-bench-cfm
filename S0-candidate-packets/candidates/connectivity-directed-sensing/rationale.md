## Why care even if duplicated?
The output would be a bounded regime/replication result with controlled explanations, not a novelty-dependent “better model” task. Positive: benefit concentrates in bottleneck-relevant layouts. Negative/null: visibility or observation cost explains the advantage, identifying when topology machinery is unnecessary. Reversal: connectivity entropy wastes probes on disconnected/unactionable alternatives. Equality only follows from a prespecified equivalence analysis, not p>0.05.
